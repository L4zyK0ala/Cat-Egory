# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md for the private reporting
channel). Maintainers will review every report, keep reporters' identities confidential and may remove,
edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors, according to the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
